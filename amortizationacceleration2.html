💰 Income Deposits (Applied on Their Actual Frequency)

Deposits hit the HELOC like a checking account: weekly = every 7 days, bi-weekly = every 14 days, monthly = every 30 days.

📊 Monthly Expenses

Expenses withdraw once per month on day 30 (after deposits that day). Do not include your mortgage payment here.

🏠 Mortgage

Mortgage interest accrues monthly (30-day months). Standard mortgage payment is applied monthly on the selected day.

💳 HELOC (Interest-Only)

HELOC interest accrues daily and is capitalized onto the HELOC balance. Chunk cycling rule: Only take a new chunk after the HELOC is paid back down to $0 (or very close to $0).
